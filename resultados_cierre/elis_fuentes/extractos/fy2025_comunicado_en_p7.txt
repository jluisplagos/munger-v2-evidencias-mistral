New cash allocation policy 
On March 6, 2025, the Group presented a capital allocation policy aimed at improving shareholder returns: 
o 
Continued pursuit of bolt-on acquisitions, with an annual budget of between 50 million euros and 150 
million euros; 
o 
Maintenance of Elis’ investment-grade status and continued reduction of the Group’s leverage, limited 
to approximately -0.1x per year; 
o 
Use of the remaining cash primarily to enhance shareholder returns, through dividend payments or 
share buybacks. 
As part of the implementation of this capital allocation policy, Elis launched a 150 million euros share 
buyback program in 2025.
