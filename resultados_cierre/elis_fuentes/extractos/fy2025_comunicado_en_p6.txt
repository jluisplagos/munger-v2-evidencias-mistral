Net capex 
In 2025, the Group’s net capex were stable compared to 2024 and stood at 18.2% as a percentage of 
revenue, compared to 19.2% in 2024. This evolution resulted from a better management of linen investment, 
as well as a favourable pricing dynamic for these purchases. The decrease is partially offset by the increase 
in leases.
