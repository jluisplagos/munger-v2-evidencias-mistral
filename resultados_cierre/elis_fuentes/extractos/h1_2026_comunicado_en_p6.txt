Net capex 
In H1 2026, the Group's net capex increased by 47.3 million euros compared with H1 2025. 
As a percentage of revenue, the ratio stood at 19.5% in H1 2026 (versus 18.4% in H1 2025), reflecting the 
ramp up of major industrial investment projects during the half. The ratio is expected to be around 18% by 
year-end. 
Change in working capital requirement  
In H1 2026, the change in working capital requirement was sharply negative, at -164.0 million euros, 
reflecting, on the one hand, an increase in flat linen inventories (ahead of the Hospitality season) and 
workwear inventories (to improve service quality), and, on the other hand, a deterioration in DSO, to 57
