## 1. La guía cambió: titular y frases literales

Titular del comunicado del 5-nov-2026 (sección inicial):

> "Zoetis Announces Third Quarter 2026 Results; Highlights Progress Advancing Innovation Across Diversified Portfolio and **Narrows Full Year 2026 Revenue Guidance, Raises Full Year 2026 Adjusted EPS Guidance**" (11/05/2026)

Frases literales de la sección «FULL YEAR 2026 FINANCIAL GUIDANCE» del comunicado del **5-nov-2026**:

> "Narrowed revenue to a range of $9.220 billion to $9.320 billion (organic operational growth of (2)% to (1)%)"
>
> "Raised adjusted net income to a range of $2.600 billion to $2.640 billion (organic operational growth of (7)% to (5)%)"
>
> "Raised adjusted diluted EPS to a range of $6.25 to $6.35"
>
> "This guidance reflects foreign exchange rates as of October 20, 2026."

La guía del **6-ago-2026** (columna «Full Year 2026 as of August 6, 2026 (Prior Guidance)» de la tabla «2026 GUIDANCE» del mismo comunicado) era, literalmente: ingresos «$9,120 to $9,320», crecimiento orgánico operacional «(3)% to (1)%», BPA ajustado «$6.15 to $6.25», renta neta ajustada «$2,570 to $2,620».
