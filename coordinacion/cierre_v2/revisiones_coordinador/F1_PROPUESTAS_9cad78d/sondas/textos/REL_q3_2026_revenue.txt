                                                                                Press release – October 28, 2026

                              Growth accelerates in Q3 2026: revenue up 6.1%
                             Strong commercial momentum across all markets
                                    Sequential improvement in organic growth
                                          2026 financial objectives confirmed

Q3 2026 Group revenue reached 1,323.9 million euros, up 6.1% on a reported basis, including 3.5% organic
growth, a 1.5% contribution from acquisitions and a 1.1% positive currency effect
o     Commercial momentum remains strong across all geographies, reflecting the Group’s efforts in recent
      years to enhance the effectiveness of its sales force
o     Gradual rollout of major contracts signed in H1, contributing to the sequential improvement in organic
      growth across all European regions
o     Strong summer season in Hospitality in Southern Europe and Scandinavia; in France, activity was
      adversely affected by wildfires in July
o     Elis’ diversified service offering and customer portfolio help mitigate the impact of a persistently
      subdued economic environment in Europe
o     In Mexico, the reallocation of volumes within the federal healthcare system weighed on performance
      throughout the quarter, in line with expectations and without any additional volume losses since June
o     Recent acquisitions contributed 1.5 percentage points to revenue growth; the pipeline remains strong
      for the remainder of the year and 2027
2026 financial objectives confirmed
o     Full-year organic revenue growth expected to be slightly below the 2025 level1
o     Adjusted EBITDA and adjusted EBIT margins expected to improve slightly
o     Free cash flow expected to grow at a mid-single-digit rate
o     Diluted headline net income per share expected to grow at a high-single-digit rate
o     Financial leverage ratio expected to decrease by approximately 0.1x as of 31 December 2026


Puteaux, 28 October 2026 – Elis, a global leader in circular services at work, today announces its revenue
for the third quarter of 2026. The figures presented in this press release have not been audited.
Commenting on the announcement, Xavier Martiré, Chairman of the Management Board of Elis, said:
“In the third quarter, Elis' revenue increased by 6.1%, including 3.5% organic growth, marking an
improvement compared with previous quarters against a generally subdued economic backdrop and
amid political uncertainty in certain countries.
This performance confirms the Group's strong commercial momentum across all geographies, reflecting
the investments made in recent years to enhance sales force effectiveness, through both increased
headcount and improved sales tools. The gradual rollout of major contracts signed in the first half is
contributing to the sequential improvement in our organic growth. Hospitality activity also benefited from
a strong summer season in Southern Europe and Scandinavia, while activity in France was adversely
affected by wildfires in July.
In Latin America, growth continues to be supported by strong commercial momentum, particularly in Brazil,
as well as price adjustments reflecting labour cost inflation. In Mexico, however, performance was affected
by the reallocation of volumes within the federal healthcare system, the impact of which was felt
throughout the quarter, in line with our expectations.
Finally, acquisitions contributed 1.5 percentage points to reported revenue growth in the third quarter,
reflecting the continued execution of our targeted acquisition strategy, enabling us to further strengthen
our local footprint. Several additional opportunities are currently under review in countries where the Group
already operates.
The Group's strong third-quarter performance enables us to confirm our financial objectives for 2026. More
fundamentally, the resilience of our circular business model across economic cycles, the efficiency of our
operational organisation and the diversification of our customer base and product portfolio are key
strengths that will continue to support our development and reinforce our leadership across our markets.”

 France
 Q3 revenue increased by 2.6%, including 2.1% organic growth, in line with the previous two quarters.
 The Group maintained strong commercial momentum despite a subdued economic environment,
 characterised by an increase in business failures and some customer hesitation against the backdrop of
 the political calendar.
 In Hospitality, wildfires in the Bordeaux region and southern France, together with heatwaves, adversely
 affected business at certain customer sites in July and August, resulting in an estimated revenue shortfall of
 approximately 2 million euros for the Group compared with 2025. September, by contrast, was particularly
 strong, benefiting notably from events that helped boost tourist activity.
 Finally, the acquisition of Muller contributed 0.5 percentage points to reported quarterly revenue growth.
 Central Europe
 Revenue in the region increased by 8.5% in Q3, including 4.5% organic growth.
 The Benelux countries and Poland maintained their positive momentum, while the Czech Republic, Slovakia
 and Hungary recorded double-digit organic growth. These strong performances were driven by continued
 momentum in new workwear contract wins.
 In Germany, organic growth was encouraging, with major Healthcare contracts signed in 2026 expected
 to be rolled out over the coming months.
 Finally, the acquisitions of Larosé and Adrett in Germany and Wäsche Perle in Switzerland contributed 3.9
 percentage points to reported quarterly revenue growth. Integration is progressing in line with expectations,
 strengthening our local networks.

 Scandinavia & Eastern Europe
 Revenue in the region increased by 3.2% in Q3, including 1.8% organic growth, and benefited from a 1.3%
 positive currency effect.
 Activity remained strong in the Baltic countries, Finland and Norway, driven by new contract wins in
 workwear. Activity was more subdued in Denmark and Sweden during the quarter, although Hospitality
 benefited from a strong summer season.
 UK & Ireland
 Revenue in the region increased by 3.5% in Q3, including 2.9% organic growth and a 0.7% positive currency
 effect.
 The macroeconomic environment remained subdued across the region, although commercial
 performance was satisfactory during the quarter, particularly in Hospitality. The Group also maintains its
 pricing discipline and takes a selective approach to acquiring new customers.
 In Ireland, competition remained particularly intense in Hospitality.
 Latin America
 Revenue in the region increased by 15.0% in Q3, benefiting notably from a positive currency effect of 8.8%.
 Organic growth stood at 3.6%.
 Brazil performed particularly well, supported by strong commercial momentum and the appeal of the
 Group's service offering. Growth in the region also continued to benefit from a significant pricing
 contribution, reflecting labour cost inflation.
 In Mexico, as previously communicated, the reallocation of volumes within the federal healthcare system
 has resulted in a loss of 50% of the volumes concerned since June, representing approximately 2 million
 euros per month. The situation has remained unchanged since then, with no recovery in volumes and no
 further losses. Following an impact limited to one month in Q2, this volume reduction affected the full third
 quarter.
 Finally, the acquisitions of Acquaflash and ServBrasil in Brazil, together with Lescen in Mexico, contributed
 2.6 percentage points to reported quarterly revenue growth.
 Southern Europe
 Revenue in the region increased by 8.0% in Q3, including 6.7% organic growth, supported by a very strong
 summer season in Hospitality and the continued trend towards outsourcing in Workwear.
 All three countries in the region delivered strong organic growth.
 Finally, the acquisition of RS10 in Spain, whose revenue has been consolidated since 1 June 2026,
 contributed 1.3 percentage points to reported quarterly revenue growth.
 Others
 The “Others” category comprises the Group's manufacturing entities (including Le Jacquard Français, a
 French designer and manufacturer of household linen, and Kennedy Hygiene, a UK manufacturer of
 washroom equipment), holding companies and operations in Asia (including Malaysia and Singapore).
 Strong organic growth notably reflects the continued development of our workwear and ultra-clean
 activities in Asia.

II. Other information
 Financial definitions
 The indicators presented below are not defined under IFRS standards and may not be comparable with
 those used by other companies.
 o   Organic growth in the Group’s revenue is calculated excluding (i) the impacts of changes in the scope
     of consolidation of “major acquisitions” and “major disposals” (as defined in the Universal Registration
     Document) in each of the periods under comparison, as well as (ii) the impact of exchange rate
     fluctuations.
 o   Adjusted EBITDA is defined as adjusted EBIT before depreciation and amortization net of the portion of
     grants transferred to income.
 o   Adjusted EBITDA margin is defined as adjusted EBITDA divided by revenue.
 o   Adjusted EBIT is defined as net income (loss) before net financial income (loss), income tax, share in net
     income of equity-accounted companies, amortization of intangible assets recognized in a business
     combination, goodwill impairment losses, other operating income and expenses, miscellaneous
     financial items (bank fees recognized in operating income) and IFRS 2 expense (share-based
     payments).
 o   Adjusted EBIT margin is defined as adjusted EBIT divided by revenue.
