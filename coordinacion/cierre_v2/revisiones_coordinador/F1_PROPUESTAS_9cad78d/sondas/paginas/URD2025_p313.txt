These alternative performance measures are meant to facilitate the analysis of Elis’s operating trends, financial performance and financial

results. These indicators, generally, have no standardized meaning and therefore may not be compared to similarly labeled indicators used
by other companies. As a result, none of these indicators should be considered separately from, or as a substitute for, the Group’s
consolidated financial statements and related notes prepared in accordance with IFRS.
